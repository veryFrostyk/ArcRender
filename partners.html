IFA Berlin 2026 Launch Partner RayNeo GT Max AR Glasses
Paid Showcase
The brief

RayNeo, a TCL subsidiary and global AR glasses brand, reached out ahead of their IFA Berlin 2026 launch. The ask was a long-form showcase covering the GT Max, a flagship AR glasses product making its global debut at one of the world's largest consumer electronics shows.

Rather than a short segment buried inside another video, ArcRender pitched a dedicated video built entirely around a genuine month of daily use. RayNeo agreed. The result was a comprehensive, experience-driven showcase that covered gaming, home cinema use, travel, and everyday portability from the perspective of someone who was genuinely converted by the product.

"I ended up giving the RayNeo GT Max its own dedicated video instead of the short integration I originally planned."

Why it worked

The video was built around a genuine personal experience: a creator who did not care about AR glasses being won over by daily use. That arc resonates with viewers because it is true. ArcRender does not produce sponsored reviews. Sponsored reviews are a conflict of interest. Every paid partnership on this channel is a showcase of honest experience, and the audience knows that. That credibility is what makes the content convert.

Partnership Terms

How ArcRender works with brands.

Straightforward terms established upfront. No surprises, no scope creep, no manufactured enthusiasm for products that are not a genuine fit.

What works
  • + Paid showcases within existing long-form videos
  • + Products that genuinely fit the channel's tech and gadget focus
  • + Review units for larger electronics with full editorial independence
  • + Direct brand contact only, no agencies
  • + Segment review for factual accuracy before publish
  • + Permission to boost published content for agreed period
What does not work
  • x Sponsored reviews — these are a conflict of interest to honest content
  • x Gifting-only collaborations with no compensation
  • x Dedicated sponsored videos or Shorts
  • x Talent agency or management inquiries
  • x Script approval or editorial control over content
  • x Products outside the channel's core interest areas
Review Units for Larger Products

For significant electronics launches including laptops, cameras, phones, handhelds, and other hardware, ArcRender is open to accepting review units under the condition that all commentary, opinions, and conclusions remain fully independent. No exceptions.

All thoughts expressed in ArcRender videos are the creator's own. A review unit does not guarantee coverage, a positive showcase, or any specific framing of the product.
